# Getting started

On a fresh install the setup wizard collects company details, people and how to start. Sample data provides a warehouse, supplier, carrier and three items, with no stock or transactions. Starting empty also asks for warehouses, vendors, items and opening stock. Opening stock is a financial entry against Opening Balance Equity.

After setup, tap your name on Who's working? The job (clerk, accounting or admin) selects the Home view and most useful navigation. It is a profile choice, with no password. To change person, open the avatar menu at the top right and choose Switch person. Show tips again restores first-use tips. Appearance offers Match this device, Light and Dark.

The normal purchase flow is Purchase order → Vendor bill → Warehouse receipt. Orders commits to buy and posts no money. On Orders, New opens a form with Vendor, Deliver to, Item, Qty and Unit $. Create order saves it. Each order tracks billing, payment and receiving separately. Open an order to see its lines and related documents; Enter the vendor's bill prepares its bill.

Receive is the dock workflow. The clerk counts physical goods; accounting reviews Bills and posts their cost. If goods arrive first they remain awaiting bill, not sellable or valued. Posting the draft bill lands the counted units automatically. If the bill is posted first, the stock sits in transit until received.

On phones the bottom tabs show the main tasks for your job. The help assistant can offer Take me there for other screens. How it works explains the document flow. The assistant explains and navigates only; use the app yourself to inspect records or perform actions.
