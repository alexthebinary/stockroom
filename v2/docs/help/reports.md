# Reports

Reports groups tabs by Accounting, Sales, Purchases and Inventory. Sales has no reports here yet; do not promise a sales dashboard.

Accounting contains Books check, Trial balance and Journal entries. Books check compares total debits with credits, inventory on hand against the stock cost pools item by item, inventory in transit against billed units not yet landed, and Accounts Payable against open vendor bills. Read its reported problems rather than assuming the books balance. Trial balance lists account balances and debit/credit totals. Journal entries lets you inspect the financial entries and their lines.

Purchases contains Awaiting bill and To receive. Awaiting bill shows goods counted at the dock that need a posted bill, oldest first; its estimates are not booked inventory value. To receive lists open orders with outstanding units, their warehouse, vendor and age. Billed units not yet received are inventory in transit. Unbilled expected units have no posted inventory value.

Inventory contains Stock value, showing on-hand quantities, held quantities, average cost, stock value, ledger value and any variance. Average cost is shared across all warehouses for each item. Units awaiting bill are held outside the valued pool. The ledger's on-hand role is used for this comparison, rather than the whole 1200 balance, which also includes inventory in transit.

Payables has the vendor aging view (current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days late); open a bill there for payments. The assistant can navigate to Reports or Payables, but cannot read totals, diagnose a specific entry or run a report against your company records.
