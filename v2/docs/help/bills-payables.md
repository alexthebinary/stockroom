# Bills and payables

Bills is accounting's inbox. To review lists drafts; Unpaid lists posted bills with an open balance; Unknown items is the dock's unmatched scans; All lists all bills. Open a draft to review the vendor, vendor invoice number, dates, quantities, prices, discounts and shipping. Save for later saves the draft. Discard draft removes an unposted draft. Hold to post bill posts after the hold completes; a valid vendor, invoice number and positive total are required.

Posting an inventory bill debits Inventory Asset and credits Accounts Payable at landed cost: purchase price less discounts plus allocated freight. Units already counted at the dock move to on hand automatically; billed units not received stay in transit. A posted bill with units still outstanding offers Receive these to open receiving for its order.

Freight bill on Bills opens the carrier invoice form. Freight-in adds to the cost of the orders it brought in. Freight-out is shipping to customers and is an expense. Freight-in affects in-transit units, on-hand average cost, or cost of goods sold if units have already left. Review the allocation and posting preview before posting.

Payables groups posted unsettled bills by vendor and due date. We owe, Past due and Vendors summarize it. Open a bill number, then Record payment. Enter Amount $, Method and Date; Record payment saves a full or partial payment. The bill's Open figure is total less paid and credited. If it is negative, Vendor owes us appears and Record vendor refund records money received back.

Void… offers a hold-to-void confirmation on a posted bill. It reverses the entry and moves landed stock back to awaiting bill with a fresh draft. Paid, credited or already-used stock prevents voiding; use the appropriate vendor credit workflow instead. The assistant can explain these steps but cannot pay, post, void or inspect a bill.
