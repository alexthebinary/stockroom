# Returns

A customer sends back goods from a shipped order. Open the order (Sales → Orders) → **Return items**.

For each line: how many are coming back, and their condition — **Back on the shelf** (choose the warehouse) or **Damaged — write off**. Give a reason. Choose where the refund goes. **Record return**.

What happens:
- Back on the shelf: stock goes up at the cost those units originally left at.
- Written off: stock does not come back; its cost stays in cost of goods sold.
- The customer is credited the returned items' price plus their share of the order's tax (shipping is not refunded).
- If they had already paid more than they now owe, that difference is refunded to the chosen method. If they had not paid, the credit simply reduces what they owe.

A line can be returned in several parts, never more than shipped. Returns are listed on the order. Serial-tracked items cannot be returned here yet.
