# Settings

**Categories (/catalogs/categories)** — two levels of product category.

**Managers (/catalogs/employees)** — names printed on orders as the responsible manager. This is a label, not a login.

**Users and roles** — people who sign in, each with a role (Viewer, Warehouse, Finance, Admin). An admin can act as a lesser role to see what that person sees. See [[getting-started]].
