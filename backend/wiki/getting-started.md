# Getting started

**Signing in.** During the beta the site password is the only thing to type; the app then signs you in as the administrator automatically.

**The menu** (left on a desktop, a bottom bar and a drawer on a phone) is grouped by the work: Operations (Home, Showroom sale, Receive, Inventory), Sales, Purchases, Finance (Month-end close, Accounting, Reports), Settings. A number next to a menu item is work waiting there; orange means urgent.

**Home (/)** opens on "Needs attention": the few things to do next, each with the button that does it — short purchase orders to receive, unpaid invoices, stock below its reorder point, shipments with no delivery confirmed.

**Search** (top of the menu, or Ctrl/⌘ K) finds anything by the number on it: SKU, PO, order, invoice, serial, customer, vendor.

**Live Audit** (bottom of the menu) reads "passing" when every ledger check holds right now. "Failing" means something in the books needs fixing — open it to see what. See [[accounting]].

**The assistant** (the sparkle button, bottom right) explains any screen, finds records and prepares work. It never changes anything by itself: every change is a card you approve. You can attach a photo, e.g. a delivery's packing slip — see [[receiving]].

**Roles.** Viewer (read only), Warehouse (stock: receive, pack, ship, adjust), Finance (money: invoices, payments, bills, the ledger), Admin (everything, plus users). Buttons you cannot use are shown greyed with the reason.

**About (/about)** explains how the data fits together, read live from the database.
