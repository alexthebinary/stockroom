# Showroom sale

For a client standing in the showroom who pays and takes the goods now. Operations → **Showroom sale** (/showroom).

1. Pick the warehouse, search, and tap **+** on each product (it will not add more than is available).
2. Client: Walk-in, New client (name, optional email/phone — they join the customer list), or Existing client.
3. Payment method (Card, Cash, Bank transfer) and sales tax %.
4. **Take payment** — one step: the order is created, stock leaves at FIFO cost, the invoice is raised and marked paid, and the order is marked collected/delivered.
5. **Open invoice (PDF)** to print or send the paid in-store invoice.

Serial-tracked products cannot be sold here yet; use a normal sales order. Returns from a showroom sale work like any other: [[returns]].
